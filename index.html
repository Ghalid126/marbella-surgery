About Dr. Dhobb

Dr. Dhobb is a board-certified surgeon with a specific interest in the treatment of varicose veins, spider veins, and thread vein removal.

He has been committed to the treatment of venous insufficiency for over 20 years, offering the most contemporary minimally-invasive techniques to his patients.

He received his medical degree from the Faculty of Medicine of Montpellier, France, and further trained in general and vascular disease at Montpellier and Paris University Hospitals. He is a fellow of the French College of Vascular Surgeons, fully registered with Spanish authorities, and holds a full qualification in Angiology from Paris as well as a University diploma in Endovascular Surgery.

Dr. Dhobb is the past Vice President of the International Union of Angiology, an international lecturer, and has published many articles on vascular treatment. He also completed a University Master in Aesthetic Medicine to offer patients a complete range of well-being and beauty enhancement.

Our Specialties

Advanced Venous Treatments

State-of-the-art minimally invasive procedures with the latest technology and an immediate return to normal activities.

Varicose Veins

Comprehensive treatment of enlarged, twisted veins using the most advanced minimally invasive techniques. We address both cosmetic concerns and symptomatic venous insufficiency.

Endovenous Laser (EVLA)

Pioneering endovenous laser ablation in Spain. A minimally invasive procedure that uses laser energy to close varicose veins, with excellent cosmetic results and fast recovery.

Spider & Thread Veins

Effective removal of spider veins and thread veins using sclerotherapy, microsclerotherapy, and vascular laser treatments for smooth, clear skin.

Sclerotherapy

Ultrasound-guided sclerotherapy and foam sclerotherapy — highly effective techniques for treating varicose veins and spider veins without surgery.

Facial & Hand Veins

Facial veins erased by precision vascular laser. Hand veins treated with microsurgical techniques for natural-looking results.

Aesthetic Medicine

In addition to venous treatments, Dr. Dhobb holds a University Master in Aesthetic Medicine. Our clinic offers beauty enhancements including radiofrequency skin tightening, IPL photofacial, laser treatments, dermal fillers, botulinum toxin, skin resurfacing, and ZO® Skin Health products — all favouring a natural look using minimally invasive techniques.

Your Journey

The Patient Experience

From your first consultation to complete recovery, we ensure a seamless, comfortable experience with the highest standards of care.

1

Initial Consultation

A thorough assessment of your venous condition using advanced ultrasound venous exploration. Dr. Dhobb personally evaluates each patient to determine the nature and extent of venous insufficiency.

2

Personalised Treatment Plan

Based on your diagnosis, Dr. Dhobb creates a specific treatment plan to produce optimal results with the least discomfort and downtime. All options are explained clearly.

3

In-Clinic Treatment

Treatments are performed in our state-of-the-art clinic in Marbella. Using the latest approved technology, procedures are minimally invasive with local anaesthesia.

4

Recovery & Follow-Up

Return home the same day and resume normal activities immediately. We provide comprehensive follow-up care to ensure the best possible outcome for every patient.
