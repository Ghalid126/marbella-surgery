About Dr. Dhobb

A Legacy of Vascular Excellence

Dr. Dhobb is a board-certified Surgeon with a specific interest in the treatment of varicose veins, spider veins, venous ulcers, and thread vein removal. He has been committed to the treatment of venous insufficiency for over 20 years, offering the most contemporary minimally-invasive techniques to his patients.

He received his medical degree from the Faculty of Medicine of Montpellier, France, and further trained in general and vascular disease at Montpellier and Paris University Hospitals. He is a fellow of the French College of Vascular Surgeons, fully registered with Spanish authorities, and holds a full qualification in Angiology from Paris as well as a University diploma in Endovascular Surgery.

Dr. Dhobb is the past Vice President of the International Union of Angiology, an international lecturer, and has published many articles on vascular treatment. He also completed a University Master in Aesthetic Medicine to offer patients a complete range of well-being and beauty enhancement.

Our Specialties

Advanced Vascular Treatments

State-of-the-art minimally invasive procedures with the latest technology, performed in our clinic with immediate return to normal activities.

Varicose Veins

Comprehensive treatment of enlarged, twisted veins using the most advanced minimally invasive techniques. We address both cosmetic concerns and symptomatic venous insufficiency.

Endovenous Laser (EVLA)

Pioneering endovenous laser ablation in Spain. A minimally invasive procedure that uses laser energy to close varicose veins, with excellent cosmetic results and fast recovery.

Spider & Thread Veins

Effective removal of spider veins and thread veins using sclerotherapy, microsclerotherapy, and vascular laser treatments for smooth, clear skin.

Sclerotherapy

Ultrasound-guided sclerotherapy and foam sclerotherapy — highly effective techniques for treating varicose veins and spider veins without surgery.

Facial & Hand Veins

Facial veins erased by precision vascular laser. Hand veins treated with microsurgical techniques for natural-looking results.

Vascular Surgery

Ambulatory microphlebectomy and reconstructive vascular surgery. Expert operative techniques for complex vascular conditions, performed as outpatient procedures.

Aesthetic Medicine

In addition to vascular surgery, Dr. Dhobb holds a University Master in Aesthetic Medicine. Our clinic offers beauty enhancements including radiofrequency skin tightening, IPL photofacial, laser treatments, dermal fillers, skin resurfacing, and ZO® Skin Health products — all favouring a natural look using minimally invasive techniques.
